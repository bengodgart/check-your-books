Check Your Books

Paste or upload an invoice export and get a ranked data-quality report. The tool finds the duplicates, outliers, broken references, contradictions, and bad dates that make a book untrustworthy, then explains each one in plain English. It runs entirely in your browser. No account, and nothing leaves this page.

Settings and API key DEMO mode

You do not need a key to try this. Load the sample and click Audit and you get the full report offline. The detection is real either way: the duplicates, outliers, broken references, and bad dates are found by deterministic code, not guessed.

In DEMO mode the finding wording ships prepared. Add your own Anthropic API key to switch to LIVE mode, where the page calls Claude directly from your browser to write the finding wording and weigh in on severity. Your key is stored only in this browser, in localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server of ours, because there is no server.

LIVE mode model: . A single audit costs a few cents at most, billed to your own Anthropic account. A cheaper model works too if you switch the constant in the kernel.
